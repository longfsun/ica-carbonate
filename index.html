Data fields, interpretation and loading

I/Ca is displayed in μmol/mol; Mg/Ca in mol/mol; δ¹³C and δ¹⁸O in ‰ as supplied by the source CSV. Isotope reference scales are not specified in this file: consult the original publications. Age is in Ma before present.

Missing or non-numeric measurements are omitted from the corresponding chart and are never converted to zero. Zero values are retained. No smoothing, outlier removal, duplicate removal or proxy conversion is applied. All measurements use the same marker color; source references are available on hover.

This portal displays reported measurements rather than a direct quantitative reconstruction of seawater oxygen concentrations. Consult each publication for analytical methods, age models, carbonate mineralogy and screening criteria.
